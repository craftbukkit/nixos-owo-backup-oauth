Terms of use

NixOS OWO Backup is a personal, locally operated backup configuration. This website provides information about that configuration and its Google OAuth access.

Private use

The configuration is intended for its operator’s own computer and Google account. It is not a public backup service, and no public registration, subscription, storage allocation, or support service is offered through this site.

The operator must have the right to access and copy the files selected for backup and must maintain the permissions and available storage of the destination account.

What the configuration does

The utility performs one-way copying to Google Drive. Its current and historical files are retained separately, and local deletion does not instruct the workflow to delete the corresponding cloud backup.

Transfer completion depends on the local computer, file stability, network availability, Google API availability, upload queues, and available storage. The operator should review failed or pending files and verify recovery from the actual backup. This website does not certify that a particular backup or restore has completed.

Account access and retained copies

The operator can stop the local service and revoke the utility’s Google permission. Existing cloud files, historical versions, local reports, and downloaded restores remain until the operator separately removes them. See the privacy policy for retention and deletion details.

Other services and projects

Use of Google Drive remains subject to Google’s terms and any applicable Drive terms. GitHub’s terms apply to the hosting of these information pages. The backup configuration uses rclone on NixOS; those projects retain their own licenses and terms.
